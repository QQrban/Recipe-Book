# Pancakes

## Ingredients

- Flour
- Milk
- Eggs
- Sugar
- Salt
- Butter

## Instructions

1. Mix flour, sugar, and salt.
2. Add eggs and milk.
3. Mix until smooth.
4. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
5. Pour some batter into the pan.
6. Cook on both sides until golden.
7. Serve with your favorite toppings.
